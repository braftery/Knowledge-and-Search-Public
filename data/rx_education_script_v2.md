## Opening

Greeting, your own words:

Thanks for calling Harbor Health. This is [first name] on the pharmacy education line. You are calling about a prescription on a Medicare Advantage plan, and this call may be monitored. What can I help you with?

Advocate note: Skip the long intro if they already told the IVR the drug. Just confirm spelling of the drug and which pharmacy they want to use.

## Verification

Ask for the member ID plus either date of birth or address. Caregivers need an authorization on the account before any drug or price talk. Unverified callers get a callback offer only.

## What to say about the price

Read word for word before any dollar figure.

Verbatim (Legal): The price I quote is an estimate for a 30-day supply at a preferred retail pharmacy, using the plan records I can see today. What you pay can change if the pharmacy, the days' supply, or your stage of coverage is different. This estimate is not a guarantee of payment.

Advocate note: If they ask whether that number is the copay, say it reflects what the plan record shows for copay or coinsurance. Do not promise the register price.

## Mail order and specialty

Policy. For a maintenance drug, offer a 90-day mail-order supply on the first fill. The member does not need any retail fills first.

Specialty drugs stay at Harbor Specialty Pharmacy and are not converted to mail order on this call.

## Closing

Verbatim (Legal): Is there anything else I can help you with today? Thank you for calling Harbor Health. We appreciate you trusting us with your coverage.

A call summary shows in the member portal within two business days. Add that in your own words only, with no extra price or network comment.
