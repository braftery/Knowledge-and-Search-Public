## Purpose

This SOP is the working guide for the specialty intake desk on the Harbor Medicare Advantage pharmacy line. It describes how the desk opens a case, how the desk speaks about cost, and how a finished packet reaches Harbor Specialty Pharmacy.

Harbor Health opened the specialty desk after the same new prescription was bouncing between pharmacy education and a clinical pharmacist, with no single form for the prescriber and the medicine. This page replaces the binders team leads used to keep. The education line and the specialty desk still share a phone menu, which is why the identity check is written at the front of this page.

## Scope

The SOP covers specialty medicines that Harbor Specialty Pharmacy ships for Harbor Medicare Advantage members, including injectables and high-touch oral products. Retail maintenance fills, medical visit estimates, and benefits appeals sit in other guides. Infusion chair scheduling and home nursing visits are outside this page. The page also does not set dollar limits that live on the specialty rate card.

## Roles

Three groups are named on a specialty intake: the pharmacy advocate on the call, the specialty pharmacist, and the team lead. Member Experience and the quality program sit outside this SOP.

The Pharmacy advocate is the only role this SOP authorizes to quote a specialty cost on the intake call.

## Before you start

In this SOP, intake means the form that opens a specialty pharmacy case.

In this SOP, a window means the return-call time already promised to the member.

This section holds the identity check and the referral check. They are written at the front of the page because of the shared phone menu described above.

Confirm the caller's identity with two identifiers chosen from the member ID, the date of birth, and the address on file.

Until the caller is verified, do not give a copay, a tier, or a coverage limit, and do not say whether a drug is on the formulary.

A caregiver who is not listed as an authorized caller may set a callback time only.

Check that a specialty referral from the prescriber is posted on the account before you open the intake form.

## Intake steps

This section is the packet. The lines below are the fields the form asks for and the handoff that follows them.

Record the prescriber name, the medicine name, and the diagnosis code on the intake form before you save it.

Send the finished intake packet to Harbor Specialty Pharmacy before the end of the same business day.

Read the specialty cost wording word for word before you say any dollar amount.

## Cost

Cost talk uses two blocks. The first block is the specialty wording. The second block is the retail comparison wording. Figures in this section are illustrative.

Verbatim (Legal): The specialty cost I quote is an estimate for the supply and the pharmacy on the plan record today. Your cost can change if the supply, the pharmacy, or your coverage stage is different. This estimate is not a guarantee of payment.

The figure I give is an estimate for a 30-day supply at a preferred retail pharmacy, taken from the plan records I can see today. What the member pays can change if the pharmacy, the days' supply, or the stage of coverage is different. The estimate is not a guarantee of payment.

The illustrative specialty intake copay at a preferred network pharmacy is $55.

The shipping fee for a refrigerated specialty package is on the specialty rate card. This SOP does not state that fee.

## Transfers

Members often ask, after the packet is open, whether the medicine can move to another pharmacy. Mail order and retail moves are both handled in this section.

Offer a 90-day mail-order supply for a maintenance drug only after that drug has been filled at a retail pharmacy two times in the current calendar year.

If the member asks to move a specialty medicine to a retail pharmacy, decline the transfer and keep the fill at Harbor Specialty Pharmacy.

## Exceptions and escalation

This section covers a missing signature and a missed return call. Director thresholds stay on the rate card, as the scope of this SOP already says.

When the prescriber signature is missing, escalate the intake to the specialty supervisor on the same business day.

If the advocate misses the window, make one more attempt within 2 days.

The dollar amount that sends a specialty case to the pharmacy director is printed on the specialty rate card. This SOP does not state that amount.

## Records

This section is what remains on file after the call.

Keep the intake form and the call note for 10 years from the date of the intake. This period is illustrative.
