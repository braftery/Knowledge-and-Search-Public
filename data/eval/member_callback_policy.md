## Purpose

This policy tells pharmacy advocates and insurance advocates how to promise a return call, what to say when they promise it, and what happens when the call is missed. Member Experience owns the page because both desks were making the promise in different words, and members were hearing two versions of the same commitment. The page is the one advocates train to on either queue.

Harbor started writing the policy after a month of complaints that a promised call never came, or came to a number the member had not confirmed. Quality review found the desk notes empty on those calls, so the next advocate could not see what had been promised. The policy puts the promise, the number, and the note in one place. It grew out of the member experience forum, where both desks sit, and it replaced the short cards that used to hang at each pod.

## Scope

The policy covers callbacks on the Harbor Medicare Advantage service line for pharmacy questions and for medical benefit questions. It covers the advocate who is ending the first call and the advocate who places the return call. It does not set drug prices, visit copays beyond the training example in the procedures section, appeal deadlines, or prior authorization clocks. Those stay in the pharmacy and benefits guides. Weekend coverage and holiday coverage are outside this version of the page.

## Definitions

A callback means a return phone call from Harbor Health to the member or the authorized caregiver.

A window means the block of time the advocate promised for that return call.

A desk note means the note on the member record that the next advocate can read.

## When to offer a callback

This section lists the offers this policy allows.

Offer a callback when you cannot verify the caller with two identifiers.

Offer a callback when a medical claim question needs a document that is not on the screen.

Offer a grievance callback only after the member asks to speak with a supervisor about a medical benefit.

Which plans include a nurse callback is listed on the plan benefit grid. This policy does not name the plans.

## Procedures

Figures in this section are illustrative.

Write the callback reason and the promised window in the desk note before you end the conversation.

Place the callback only to the phone number the member confirmed during this conversation.

Schedule member callbacks on weekdays between 8:00 a.m. and 6:00 p.m. Eastern. These hours are illustrative.

The training example uses an in-network specialist copay of $45. That figure is illustrative.

## What members hear

Members hear a short promise at the end of the call, and they can see a line in the portal afterward. Voicemail, when a call reaches a recording, is covered in this section as well.

Read the callback block word for word before you end the call.

Verbatim (Legal): Harbor Health will call you back on the number you just confirmed. Please keep that phone with you during the window we agreed.

Will you say why you are calling before I pick up? We say that Harbor Health is returning your call. We do not give the medicine name, the diagnosis, or a claim amount until we verify you.

Do not leave the medicine name, the diagnosis, or a claim amount on a voicemail.

You may add, in your own words, that a summary of this call will appear in the member portal within two days.

## Missed callbacks

A missed callback is a return call the member does not answer during the promised window. This section is the retry and the portal message that follows it.

If the advocate misses the window, make one more attempt within 2 business days.

If the second attempt also fails, send a portal message on the same day and close the callback task.

A missed callback does not extend a prior authorization deadline or an appeal deadline.

## Measures

This section is the completion target. The monthly pack carries wait times separately from this page. The figure is illustrative.

The desk target is to complete 90 percent of callbacks inside the promised window. This figure is illustrative.

The average number of minutes a member waits before a callback is reported in the monthly operations pack. This policy does not state that average.
