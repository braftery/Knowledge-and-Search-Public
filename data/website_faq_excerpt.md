## What price will I pay at the pharmacy?

Prices on this page are estimates for a 30-day supply at a preferred retail pharmacy, based on the plan information we have today. The amount you pay may differ if the pharmacy, the days' supply, or your stage of coverage is different. An estimate is not a guarantee of payment.

## Why did the pharmacy charge a different amount?

The pharmacy may have used a different days' supply, or your coverage stage may have changed since you looked at this page. Call the number on your member ID card and an advocate can read the current estimate.

## Who do I call?

Call Harbor Health Member Services at the number on your ID card and ask for the pharmacy education team.
