## When a member asks what a visit will cost

Say you are looking at today's plan record. Keep it short. Then read the next block word for word, before any dollar amount.

Verbatim (Legal): The cost I quote is an estimate for a covered in-network medical visit, based on the plan records I can see today. What you pay can change if the provider is out of network, if a referral is missing, or if your deductible status is different. This estimate is not a guarantee of payment.

Advocate note: Members ask "so is that all I pay?" Stop after the verbatim line. You may point at the copay or the deductible status on the record. You may not promise the clinic will bill that amount.

## Referrals

Policy. A specialist visit on a Harbor Medicare Advantage HMO needs a referral from the primary care doctor on file before the visit.

Check the account for that referral, and quote a cost only when the referral is there. The training example uses an in-network specialist copay of $35. That $35 figure is illustrative. If the member has no referral on file, offer to send the request to the primary care office and do not invent a price. The referral has to be on the account before you quote the specialist copay, and the illustrative training copay stays $35 only for that in-network example.
