## Get your medicines delivered

Save a trip to the pharmacy. With Harbor Mail Pharmacy, your medicines come to your door in discreet, temperature-controlled packaging.

## What you can get by mail

You can get up to a 90-day supply of most maintenance medicines, including insulin, delivered to your home through Harbor Mail Pharmacy.

Maintenance medicines are ones you take regularly for an ongoing condition, such as high blood pressure or diabetes.

## How to start

Ask your doctor to send a 90-day prescription to Harbor Mail Pharmacy, or call the number on your member ID card and we can contact your doctor for you.

## Shipping

Standard shipping is free. Orders usually arrive within 7 to 10 days. These timeframes are illustrative.
