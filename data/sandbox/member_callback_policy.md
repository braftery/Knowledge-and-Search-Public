## Purpose

Some questions cannot be resolved on the first call: a claim needs research, a pharmacy needs to confirm a price, or a prescriber office has not responded. This policy sets how advocates offer and schedule a callback, so members get a reliable promise and the next advocate has what they need.

## When to offer a callback

Offer a callback when the answer depends on someone outside the call and the member does not want to hold. Do not offer a callback for questions that search can answer during the call.

## Procedures

Offer a Harbor callback window on weekdays from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Eastern. These hours are illustrative.

Ask the member which part of the window works best. Callbacks are not available on weekends or Harbor holidays.

Write the callback reason in the desk note before you end the conversation.

The note should say what the member asked, what you already checked, and what the next advocate needs to confirm.

Use only the phone number the person confirmed during this conversation.

If the member wants a different number, verify it and update the contact record before you schedule the callback.

## What members hear

At the end of the call, read the callback line exactly as written.

We will call you back on the number you confirmed. Please keep that phone nearby.

## Missed callbacks

If the member does not answer, the advocate leaves a voicemail with no health details and tries once more within the same window. After two attempts the task closes and a letter is sent.

## Measures

Member Experience reviews callback completion each month. The target is 95 percent of callbacks made within the promised window. This target is illustrative.
