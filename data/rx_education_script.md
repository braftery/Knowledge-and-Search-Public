## Opening

Greeting. Say this in your own words, and keep it short.

Thanks for calling Harbor Health Member Services. My name is [first name]. I see you are calling about a prescription question on your Medicare Advantage plan. This call may be monitored or recorded. How can I help you today?

Advocate note: If the IVR already captured the drug name, do not make the member retell the whole story. Confirm the drug and the pharmacy, then move on.

## Who you are speaking with

Verify the member with two of these: member ID, date of birth, or the address on file. If a caregiver is on the line, check the authorization on the account before you talk about the drug or the price. If you cannot verify, offer to call the member back. Do not quote a copay, a tier, or a coverage limit to an unverified caller, and do not say whether a drug is on the formulary.

## Pricing the fill

Before you say any dollar amount, read the next block word for word. Do not shorten it.

Verbatim (Legal): The price I quote is an estimate for a 30-day supply at a preferred retail pharmacy, based on the plan records I can see today. Your actual cost can change if the pharmacy, the days' supply, or your stage of coverage is different. This estimate is not a guarantee of payment.

Advocate note: Members often ask "is that my copay?" after this line. You may say the estimate reflects the copay or coinsurance on the plan record. You may not promise that the pharmacy will charge that amount.

## Mail order and specialty

Policy. For a maintenance drug, offer a 90-day mail-order supply only after the member has filled that same drug at a retail pharmacy two times in the current calendar year.

Specialty drugs stay at Harbor Specialty Pharmacy and are not converted to mail order on this call.

## Closing

Read the next two sentences word for word, then stop.

Verbatim (Legal): Is there anything else I can help you with today? Thank you for calling Harbor Health. We appreciate you trusting us with your coverage.

You may add, in your own words, that a summary of this call will appear in the member portal within two business days. Do not add any price, tier, or network statement after the closing verbatim.
