# Writing

Plain, short and specific. These rules apply to every label, message and heading, from the ux-ui-design skill's copywriting rules plus the project's own.

## House rules

- No emojis. No em dashes. No sentences of the form "it's not x, it's y".
- Sentence case for everything, including buttons, tabs, headings and navigation.
- Sentences of 20 words or fewer. Front-load the key fact.
- Plain words at about a sixth-grade level. Explain an acronym the first time it appears.
- One term per concept across every surface (see the glossary).
- Numerals for numbers: "3 waiting", "18 of 20".
- Every figure on screen carries "Illustrative" nearby.

## Buttons and links

- Buttons are a verb plus a noun and make sense on their own: "Approve and publish", "Send back to author", "Flag this answer", "Search approved answers".
- Links describe their destination: "Open the source document", never "Learn more" or "Click here".
- Loading labels end with the ellipsis character: "Publishing…".

## Messages

- Success toast: what happened, in the past tense, then Undo. "Published version 2. Undo".
- Error: the problem, why, and the fix, without blame. "This unit has an open conflict. Legal must choose a wording before it can go live."
- No answer, for advocates: "No approved answer for this question. Tell the member you will follow up, and use Flag this answer if you think one should exist. The question was logged for the knowledge team."
- No answer, for members: "I don't have an approved answer for that yet. I can connect you with a member advocate." Never apologise twice and never guess.

## Member voice

Warm, direct and calm. Second person, contractions allowed. The bot never says "I think". It answers with approved wording only and names its source in plain words: "From Harbor Health drug pricing, updated Oct 12". Members never see unit IDs, version numbers, knowledge base names or the word "unit".

## Glossary

| Use | Do not use |
| --- | --- |
| unit (internal only) | snippet, article, chunk |
| approved | published, live (as a status) |
| version 2 | v2, rev 2 |
| send back | reject, decline |
| knowledge base | KB |
| Knowledge Ops author | editor, content writer |
| Legal approver | reviewer, compliance |
| advocate | agent, rep (in UI text) |
| source changed | drift (in UI text; "drift" is fine in docs) |
| no approved answer | no results, not found |
