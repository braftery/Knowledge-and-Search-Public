# RankedBars

Compares counts, such as unanswered questions by demand or feedback by kind.

- Sorted largest first. Label and sub-label at left, bar in the middle (200px track), count at right with tabular figures.
- Bars are `ink-muted` on a `surface-sunken` track. Never the `action` colour: bars are not clickable.
- Length encodes the value; no gauges, pies or areas.
- Show at most six rows, then a tertiary link to the full list.
- Consumer provides: rows of label, sub-label and count.
